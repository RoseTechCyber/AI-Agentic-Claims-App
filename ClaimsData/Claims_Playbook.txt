====================================================
CLAIMS HANDLING PLAYBOOK
Version 1.0
====================================================

PURPOSE

This playbook provides guidance for assessing claims,
detecting fraud indicators, determining claim priority,
and recommending preferred suppliers.

====================================================
SECTION 1 - CLAIM PRIORITY RULES
====================================================

Priority Levels:

HIGH
- Estimated loss exceeds 10000
- Customer is classified as VIP
- Claim involves injury or medical emergency
- Business interruption reported

MEDIUM
- Estimated loss between 5000 and 10000
- Property damage affecting normal operations

LOW
- Estimated loss below 5000
- Cosmetic damage only
- No safety concerns

====================================================
SECTION 2 - FRAUD INDICATORS
====================================================

Potential Fraud Indicators:

1. Multiple claims submitted within 12 months

2. Claim value significantly exceeds normal average

3. Missing supporting documents

4. Contradictory claim information

5. Recently activated policy

6. Supplier requested by claimant differs from approved suppliers

Fraud Risk Scoring:

0-1 Indicators = Low Risk

2-3 Indicators = Medium Risk

4+ Indicators = High Risk

====================================================
SECTION 3 - CLAIM ACTIONS
====================================================

High Priority Claims

Actions:
- Assign adjuster immediately
- Notify claims manager
- Begin supplier selection process
- Contact claimant within 4 hours

Medium Priority Claims

Actions:
- Assign adjuster
- Request documents if missing
- Contact claimant within 1 business day

Low Priority Claims

Actions:
- Queue for standard processing
- Validate supporting documents

====================================================
SECTION 4 - SUPPLIER SELECTION RULES
====================================================

Property Claims

Preferred Suppliers:
- ABC Property Repairs
- Rapid Restore Services
- Elite Home Restoration

Motor Claims

Preferred Suppliers:
- Premier Auto Assessors
- Swift Vehicle Repairs

Medical Claims

Preferred Suppliers:
- MediCare Claims Network
- HealthFirst Providers

Electronics Claims

Preferred Suppliers:
- Secure Tech Recovery

====================================================
SECTION 5 - SUPPLIER RANKING
====================================================

When multiple suppliers qualify:

Rank suppliers using:

1. Preferred Supplier Status
2. Region Match
3. Performance Rating
4. SLA Response Time
5. Cost Efficiency

Highest ranked supplier should be recommended.

====================================================
SECTION 6 - ESCALATION RULES
====================================================

Escalate to Claims Manager when:

- Fraud Risk is High
- Estimated Loss exceeds 50000
- Supplier cannot be identified
- Legal action is indicated
- Customer complaint received

====================================================
SECTION 7 - OUTPUT FORMAT
====================================================

The Claims Agent shall return:

- Claim ID
- Priority Level
- Fraud Risk
- Recommended Actions
- Recommended Supplier
- Escalation Requirement
- Reasoning Summary
